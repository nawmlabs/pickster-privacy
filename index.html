# Privacy Policy for Ask Pickster **Last updated: April 9, 2026** This Privacy Policy describes how Ask Pickster ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. --- ## 1. Information We Collect ### 1.1 Location Data We request access to your device's location solely to retrieve local weather information, which is used to provide context-aware decision suggestions. Location data is not stored on our servers and is only used in real time. ### 1.2 Account Information We support two types of accounts: - **Free users** sign in anonymously. We assign a temporary user ID to enable app features, but we do not collect any personal information. - **Paid users** sign in with their Google account via Google Sign-In. In this case, we may collect your name, email address, and Google profile picture as provided by Google. This information is used solely for account identification and is not shared with third parties beyond the services listed in Section 3. For information on how Google handles your data, please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). ### 1.3 Usage Data We store your conversation history and decision records in Firebase Firestore to enable the app's core features (history, stats, AI coaching context). This data is associated with your account — either your anonymous user ID (free users) or your Google account (paid users). ### 1.4 AI Interaction Data When you use Ask Pickster, the text you enter and your recent conversation history may be processed to generate AI-powered responses and decision suggestions. This information is used to provide the app’s core functionality, maintain context, and improve response quality and safety. AI-generated responses are automated and may occasionally be inaccurate, incomplete, or inappropriate. Your input is transmitted to OpenAI's API for processing and is subject to [OpenAI's Privacy Policy] (https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). ### 1.5 Advertising Data We use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and other device identifiers to serve personalized ads. Please refer to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for more information. ### 1.6 Purchase Data We use RevenueCat to process in-app purchases and manage subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect purchase history and device identifiers. Please refer to [RevenueCat's Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) for more information. --- ## 2. How We Use Your Information - To provide and improve the AI decision coaching features - To display personalized or contextual responses based on recent history - To restore purchases and manage subscription status - To display relevant advertisements - To send local notifications (only with your permission) - To generate AI-powered responses and decision suggestions - To monitor, maintain, and improve the quality and safety of AI-generated features --- ## 3. Data Sharing We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We share data only with the following service providers as necessary to operate the app: | Service | Purpose | Privacy Policy | |---------|---------|----------------| | Google Firebase | Authentication, database | [Link](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) | | Google AdMob | Advertising | [Link](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | | RevenueCat | In-app purchases | [Link](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy) | | OpenWeatherMap | Weather data | [Link](https://openweathermap.org/privacy-policy) | | OpenAI | AI response generation (GPT-4o mini, GPT-5 mini) | [Link](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy) | --- ## 4. Data Retention Your conversation history and account data are retained as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at the address below. --- ## 5. Children's Privacy Ask Pickster is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it. --- ## 6. Your Rights Depending on your location, you may have the right to: - Access the personal data we hold about you - Request correction or deletion of your data - Opt out of personalized advertising (via your device settings) To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below. --- ## 7. Security We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. --- ## 8. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. --- ## 9.
